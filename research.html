RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Curiosity is the starting point. Evidence is the guide.

Our R&D work explores technologies, materials, mechanisms, electronics, automation and human needs to create products that can solve practical problems.

01

Problem research

Observe users, industries and environments. Define the real problem before selecting a solution.

02

Technology exploration

Evaluate sensors, robotics, AI, embedded systems, materials, fabrication methods and emerging technologies.

03

Concept development

Generate alternative concepts and compare them for usefulness, safety, feasibility, maintainability and cost.

04

Prototype laboratory

Build experimental rigs, mock-ups, electronic prototypes and functional models for testing.

05

Validation

Measure performance against defined requirements and use findings to iterate the design.
